This Privacy Policy explains how AJ Gaming Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the games we publish on the Amazon Appstore (the "Games"). It applies to every AJ Gaming Studio game.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The Games do not ask you to create an account, do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and do not require an internet connection to play.

No advertising, no tracking

The Games contain no advertising, no third party advertising or analytics SDKs, no in-app purchases and no tracking of any kind.

Game progress

Your progress (levels, coins, settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Amazon Appstore

The Games use the Amazon Appstore license check (DRM) to confirm that your copy was obtained from the Amazon Appstore. This check is handled by the Amazon Appstore on your device and we do not receive any personal information from it. Purchases and downloads are handled by Amazon under the Amazon Privacy Notice.

Children

The Games are suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or from anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
